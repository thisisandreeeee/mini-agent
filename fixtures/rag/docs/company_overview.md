# Kestrel Analytics — Company Overview

Kestrel Analytics was founded on 12 March 2019 by Dr. Amara Voss and Tobias Renn
in Halden Bay, Oregon. The company builds real-time stream-processing software for
industrial and logistics customers.

## Facts at a glance

- Legal entity: Kestrel Analytics Incorporated.
- Headquarters: 88 Tideline Row, Halden Bay, Oregon.
- Second office: Achtergracht 41, Utrecht, Netherlands (opened September 2023).
- Headcount: 214 employees as of 1 January 2026.
- CEO: Dr. Amara Voss. CTO: Tobias Renn. CFO: Priya Kanwal (joined May 2022).
- Flagship product: Tidepool, a real-time stream analytics platform.
- Company mascot: a kestrel named "Pip".

## Funding history

- Seed round: $3.5M in July 2019, led by Basalt Seed Partners.
- Series A: $14M in November 2020, led by Ashgrove Ventures.
- Series B: $47M in June 2023, led by Ashgrove Ventures with participation from
  Meridian Growth. Post-money valuation was $310M.

## Mission statement

"Turn the firehose into a signal." Kestrel's internal north-star metric is the
"time to first insight" (TTFI), which the company aims to keep under 900 milliseconds
for every customer deployment.

The company operates on a four-day work week ("Kestrel Fridays" are off) since
January 2024.
