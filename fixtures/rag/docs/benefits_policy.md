# People & Benefits Policy (2026)

This policy applies to all full-time Kestrel Analytics employees. Contractors are
covered by a separate agreement.

## Time off

- Paid time off: 26 days per calendar year, accrued monthly.
- Kestrel Fridays: the company is closed every Friday (four-day work week).
- Sabbatical: after 4 continuous years, employees receive a fully paid 6-week
  sabbatical, called "Slack Tide".
- Bereavement leave: up to 10 days.

## Stipends

- **Wellspring** wellness stipend: $1,800 per year, reimbursable for gym, therapy,
  or ergonomic equipment.
- **Tidebook** learning budget: $2,500 per year for courses, books, and conferences.
- Home office setup: a one-time $1,200 allowance for new hires.

## Health & retirement

- Health insurance premiums are 100% covered for the employee and 80% for dependents.
- Retirement match: Kestrel matches 6% of salary, vesting immediately.

## Parental leave

- Primary caregiver: 20 weeks fully paid.
- Secondary caregiver: 12 weeks fully paid.
- A phased return ("Shallows") lets returning parents work 60% hours at full pay
  for the first month back.

## Referral bonus

- Successful engineering referrals earn a $6,000 bonus, paid after the new hire
  completes 90 days.
